## What Is CyberAware?

CyberAware is a learning experience that runs in the browser and helps students, along with anyone else who wants to learn, build safer habits online. Quiz-driven activities put common digital hazards in front of the player and challenge them to spot the red flags, with feedback delivered right away.

The emphasis is on hands-on awareness rather than abstract concepts. Players work through phishing attempts, OTP scams, QR code fraud, poor password choices, and unsafe links, and progress tracking lets them see how far they have come.

## How a Session Works

A learning session usually follows this pattern:

1. Open CyberAware in a web browser.
2. Pick one of the available quizzes or threat scenarios.
3. Examine the situation presented.
4. Choose the answer that matches safe online behavior.
5. Read the feedback displayed right away.
6. Keep going through the activities and check your progress.

The scenarios give you practice in spotting suspicious messages, risky links, dubious QR codes, OTP-related scams, and weak password habits.
